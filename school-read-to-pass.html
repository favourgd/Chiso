Reading is how you pass

Exams are written in words. Maths problems, science questions, history essays: if you cannot read and understand them, you cannot answer them. Students who read well learn faster in every subject.

Reading is not just looking at pages. Reading is thinking with a pencil in your hand.

The 5-step reading method

  1. 1. Preview. Look at the title, headings and pictures first. Know what is coming.
  2. 2. Ask. Turn each heading into a question you want answered.
  3. 3. Read. Read slowly to answer your question. Underline key words only.
  4. 4. Recall. Close the book. Say or write what you remember in your own words.
  5. 5. Review. Check what you missed. Read it again tomorrow, then at the end of the week.

Reading tips that raise marks

  • Read your notes and textbooks, not only past papers.
  • Look up every new word and write its meaning in a small notebook.
  • Read exam questions twice before you write a single word.
  • Read aloud when a topic is hard. Your ears help your brain.
  • Read a little of everything: stories, news, textbooks. Your vocabulary grows.
Why you keep going

Motivation

Motivation is the reason you start. Find yours and write it down.

  • Know your why. Maybe it is your family, your dream job, or proving doubters wrong. Keep it where you can see it.
  • Set a clear target. "Pass with a good grade in every subject" is better than "do well".
  • Picture the day results come. Imagine opening them and smiling. Let that picture pull you forward.
  • Celebrate small wins. Finished a chapter? That counts. Progress builds confidence.
  • Surround yourself well. Study with friends who also want to pass.

You do not need to be the smartest in the class. You need to be the one who keeps showing up.

Why you keep going when you don't feel like it

Discipline

Motivation comes and goes. Discipline stays. It is doing the work on the days you do not feel like it.

  • Fix a time. Same time, same place, every day. Habit beats mood.
  • Remove distractions. Phone away, silent, out of reach.
  • Start small. Just 10 minutes. Once you begin, you usually continue.
  • Plan tomorrow tonight. Write what you will read before you sleep.
  • Protect your sleep. A rested brain remembers more than a tired one.
  • Never miss twice. Missed a day? Fine. Be back the next day.

Discipline is a gift to your future self.

What you earn

Desert

"Desert" means what you deserve: the result that matches your effort. You cannot get a pass you have not earned, but you can earn it.

  • Sow, then harvest. Every page you read today is a seed. Exam day is harvest day.
  • Shortcuts do not last. Copying and guessing may help once, but knowledge stays with you for life.
  • Reward yourself. After a good study session, enjoy a treat, a walk, or time with friends. You earned it.
  • Be proud of honest results. A pass you worked for feels better than any gift.

Work first. Rest and reward after. You will get what you deserve, so deserve a lot.
